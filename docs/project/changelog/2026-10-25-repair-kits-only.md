Medicine no longer works on skeletons: nothing on a skeleton is tendable, so doctors stop tending skeleton colonists, prisoners and slaves with herbal or any other medicine, and there is no self-tend, drafted tend or needs-tending alert for them. Repair kits are the only treatment: the repair job now also seals leaks and treats tend-only conditions, a started kit is always used up, and the repairer shows the mechanitor's repair sparks and sound. 1 new self-test.
