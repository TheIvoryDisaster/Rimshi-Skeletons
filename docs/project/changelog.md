# Changelog

Newest first. Every change gets a dated row, added as a fragment file in `changelog/` (see its README); CI folds them into this table on main.

| Date | Change |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-10-25 | Workflow: Tithe's beta branch. Feature pull requests now go into `beta` and Claude merges them once CI is green; one standing pull request from beta into main lists everything on beta, and Matthew merges it after testing (that merge takes the next `vN` release). Changelog rows are now fragment files in `docs/project/changelog/` that CI folds into the table on main, so pull requests open at once never conflict over the table; after the fold, CI brings beta up to date with main. Test checklist pull requests from log checks still go straight to main. |
| 2026-10-25 | Art: skeleton repair kit and skeleton repair bed get their own textures (kit A and bed B from the round 2 ComfyUI proposals in `docs/art/proposals/round2/`), replacing the grey-tinted vanilla placeholders. The bed has south, east and north views. |
| 2026-10-25 | Skeletons heal like mechs: injury healing 0% on the skeleton frame gene, and a new Smith job where a crafter with repair kits works on a skeleton and its wounds mend while the work goes on (one kit per 20 damage, faster with Crafting). Repair kits are no longer medicine and the medicine filter is gone. Hurt skeletons only go to bed when a powered repair bed is free (or to be tended when bleeding); the repair bed is medical by default. 4 new self-tests replace 2. |
| 2026-10-25 | Gene lock: skeletons cannot go in the gene extractor, take a xenogerm (item, health tab bill, reimplant ability or absorbing one), or give a reimplant; skeleton genes never roll in genepacks (`canGenerateInGeneSet` false) and are stripped from any genepack another route makes, so the gene assembler never sees them. 6 new self-tests. |
| 2026-10-25 | Save converter for pawns: with the mod setting on (now "Convert Rim-Skeletons (Continued) factions and skeletons when a save loads"), every old skeleton becomes a human with the skeleton xenotype, keeping its injuries and prosthetics on the matching human parts, with our backstories, heads and pawn kinds; corpses too. A letter lists who was converted. Debug action "Convert old skeletons now". |
| 2026-10-24 | Repair kits and repair bed, first part: two research projects after Basic mechtech (skeleton repair kits 600, skeleton repair beds 1200 at a hi-tech bench). Skeleton repair kit (industrial medicine stats, vanilla medicine art tinted grey, steel 5 and a component at the machining table, Crafting 4); kits only tend skeletons and other medicine never does. Skeleton repair bed (hospital bed art tinted grey, AI persona core, plasteel, steel and components, 400 W) that repairs skeletons lying in it, scars too, and only skeletons can use. 6 new self-tests and bed and kit log lines. |
| 2026-10-24 | Skeleton recall array: research "skeleton recall codes" (2000, after basic mechtech, no mechanitor needed) unlocks an array that reuses the mechband antenna's art. A colonist broadcasts from it and a factionless, berserk old-empire skeleton horde drops in around it hours later, with charge rifles and lances, ultratech melee weapons and shield belts. 10% carry failsafe cores and are rarely finished off when downed; captured horde skeletons are interrogated and reprogrammed to join the colony. Skeletons are blocked from every reproduction route. 9 new self-tests. |
| 2026-10-24 | Spawning: skeletal imperium faction with the original roster, skeleton pawn kinds, player faction and "Embarking 'Skeleton'" scenario, 37 skeleton backstories and skeleton names, skeletons 1% of outlanders, pirates and the Empire (0.5% of tribes). Mod settings with "Replace Rim-Skeletons (Continued) factions when a save loads", debug action to add the imperium to an old world. Fixes: skeletons can eat fuel while the old mod is loaded, texture self-tests run on the main thread, stray relations on new genderless pawns removed and logged. |
