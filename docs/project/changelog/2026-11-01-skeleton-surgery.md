Skeletons that lost their mechanical nature core (all of them in saves loaded after the machine organs update) get it back on load, with their machine organs. Skeleton organs read as machine parts again with Vanilla Races Expanded - Android loaded, a skeleton's human organs can never be harvested, and a regrown organ comes back as its machine part. On skeletons, Anesthetize is now "temporarily power down", which puts the unit in maintenance mode for hardware modifications; any anesthetic a skeleton would get is maintenance mode.
