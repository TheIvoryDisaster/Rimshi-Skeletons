Waking a skeleton early from a soft cycle in a repair bed, or losing the bed's power, no longer drops CPU integrity to 0% or overloads the CPU: it keeps what the cycle restored so far, but only a finished cycle has a finished cycle's effects. A soft cycle without a repair bed still resets to 0% if cut short. While a skeleton soft cycles, its other needs (maintenance, hygiene, recreation and the rest) no longer drop. The skeleton repair bed heals twice as fast.
