New building: the cable fissure, the third soft cycle facility for the skeleton repair bed. A 2x3 row of black bricks built from 100 plasteel, 2 advanced components and 60 stone blocks. Before each use a researcher tunes it with a bill (4 components, 100 steel, 40 stone blocks, 250 research points of work); it draws 250 W until tuned and nothing after. A skeleton finishing a soft cycle beside a tuned fissure gets +20 carrying capacity until it uses a one-time heave: disarm one adjacent enemy and throw it carrying capacity / 10 cells, 2 to 5 damage on a different body part per cell, double for each cell a wall stops it short. One per repair bed.
