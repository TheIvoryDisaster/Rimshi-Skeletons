Skeletons no longer have human organs. Their lungs, kidneys, stomach, liver, heart, eyes, ears and nose are now cooling fans, fluid reprocessors, a capacitor, an oil filter, a fluid pump, optical sensors, auditory arrays and an IR camera, and they're called that in the health tab, surgery menus, medical bills and the combat log. Each one can be made at the skeleton workbench for the same cost as a standard skeleton limb and installed with a repair kit once the old one is lost. Skeletons in an existing save get their machine organs when it loads.
