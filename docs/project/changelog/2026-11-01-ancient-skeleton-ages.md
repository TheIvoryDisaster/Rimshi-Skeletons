Skeletons are ancient: every newly generated skeleton is 120 to 180 years old biologically and 400 to 1200 years old chronologically. Skeletons never get the human old age conditions (frail, bad back, cataracts, dementia and the rest), whatever their age. Old Rim-Skeletons pawns converted by the mod setting are raised to at least 120 biologically and keep their chronological age. New self-tests check the ages and the missing old age conditions.
