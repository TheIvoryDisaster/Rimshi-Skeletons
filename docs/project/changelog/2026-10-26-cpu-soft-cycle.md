Deathrest is now the CPU soft cycle, and the deathrest bar is CPU integrity (30 days to empty). The repair bed no longer takes chemfuel: a cycle takes 48 hours of uninterrupted power, drawing 15 W per skill level on top of a 50 W standby (400 W while repairing), and losing power or stopping early cancels it with 0% integrity and a CPU overload. Without a repair bed, "Begin soft reset" has a skeleton stand still (or hang in a rib cage) for 5 days. Finishing either flushes the skeleton's memories and costs a little skill, with a +3 (bed) or -3 (standing) thought. CPU overload: sluggish for 2 days, steady for 2, then paranoid (+10% moving, manipulation, negotiation and trade, a daily worsening mood line, violent breaks only). New rib cage (80 steel, 3 components, basic mechtech) holds a skeleton in stasis; downed skeletons are rescued to it. 12 new self-tests.
