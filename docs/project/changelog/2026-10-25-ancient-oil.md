Skeletons have no blood. Keyed on the mechanical nature gene: they never bleed, lose blood or leave blood drops and smears, cannot have hemogen extracted, and sanguophages get nothing from biting them (colonist sanguophages skip skeleton prisoners). When hit, a skeleton leaks black "ancient oil" filth where a human would drop blood, and the red hit spray plays in black. As with mechs in vanilla, doctors no longer tend skeleton injuries; the repair job handles them. 4 new self-tests.
