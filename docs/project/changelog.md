# Changelog

Newest first. Every change gets a dated row, added as a fragment file in `changelog/` (see its README); CI folds them into this table on main.

| Date | Change |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-11-13 | Builds are no longer stored as GitHub Actions artifacts, which had filled the account's storage. Main and beta builds upload their packaged zip to the project's private Azure Blob Storage container instead (kept three days), under the run ID and attempt so reruns never overwrite each other. Main still attaches its zip to the GitHub release. The Azure upload is skipped until the Azure repository variables are set. |
| 2026-11-10 | Soft cycles now carry on through a grav ship launch and landing, like a sanguophage's deathrest. A skeleton in a standing or rib cage soft reset anywhere on the ship is no longer interrupted: its reset is held during the flight and picks up again where it lands. A repair bed cycle is no longer cancelled if the bed has not got its power back in the first moments after landing. |
| 2026-11-09 | Soft cycles are no longer cut short when another pawn repairs (or otherwise works on) the skeleton; only real damage, downing or a mental break still end one. A downed skeleton whose CPU integrity runs out begins a soft reset where it lies, which slowly mends it until it can get up. Maintenance wear no longer picks the waist. |
| 2026-11-09 | Recall array hordes are a fifth smaller. Once at least half the colonists on the map are downed, the skeleton legion loses its grip and turns on itself: every skeleton still standing goes berserk and goes for the others first. |
| 2026-11-06 | The test log reader understands RimWorld 1.7 logs: stack traces under ordinary log lines are no longer reported as mod errors. |
| 2026-11-06 | Recall horde skeletons now arrive as a real raid: they belong to a hidden, permanently hostile "recalled legion" faction, are hostile already in their drop pods and attack under the normal raid AI instead of turning berserk after landing. About one in three (at least one per horde) has no corrupted protocol and can be recruited the normal way; the rest still need interrogating and reprogramming. Horde skeletons already on the map in older saves join the legion on load. |
| 2026-11-03 | A CPU soft cycle now mends a skeleton just enough to get up by the time it finishes, legs first (nothing if both legs are gone). A downed skeleton can start a soft reset where it lies from its Soft cycle button. |
| 2026-11-03 | Skeleton corpses no longer rot or desiccate into organic skeletons (they still deteriorate outdoors), and ones that already rotted turn fresh on load. The scrap skeleton remains bill now logs why it passed over a skeleton corpse (forbidden, out of range, unreachable), and its self-test checks the full ingredient check. |
| 2026-11-03 | Skeleton arms and legs now install themselves like a mechlink: a skeleton colonist right-clicks the limb, picks a side and fits it to itself, with no surgery, doctor or repair kit. Machine organs and the AI core still need surgery, and the limb install surgeries stay as a second route. |
| 2026-11-03 | Skeletons left out of maintenance now wear down: each full day at 0% maintenance, one random part other than the brain takes 2 damage as a "worn" injury, which repair kits and the repair bed fix. |
| 2026-11-03 | Fixed damaged skeletons getting an "is fully healed" message every 10 seconds while they waited for repair: their injuries no longer count as healing on their own, so the vanilla healing step (and its message) is skipped for them. |
| 2026-11-03 | On RimWorld 1.7 a skeleton's genetics screen showed vanilla "germline genes" and "xenogenes" instead of cores and chips. The screen now swaps the wording in the language data while it draws, which works on both 1.6 and 1.7. |
| 2026-11-03 | Skeletons are now immune to addiction and chemical dependency from any source, including a chemical dependency gene in a custom xenotype. Skeletons that already have an addiction lose it when the save loads. |
| 2026-11-02 | Pawns now work at the skeleton workbench. It had no work giver, so its bills were never taken; it now sits in Smithing between the fabrication bench and the machining table, and a self-test and a unit test check that every bench of ours with bills has one. |
| 2026-11-02 | Skeleton buildings moved to the Biotech architect tab: the repair bed, tuning fork, tempest reliquary, cable fissure, rib cage and skeleton workbench now sit there beside the recall array, out of the Furniture and Production tabs. A unit test keeps new skeleton buildings there. |
| 2026-11-01 | Fixes from the 2026-11-01 log scan: the CPU integrity tooltip now swaps vanilla's deathrest text last, after any other mod's patches, and its self-test names every patch on the tooltip when it fails. The cable fissure, chemical spire and tuning fork no longer raise config errors at startup (shadow and shoot-over settings). The overclock self-test allows other mods to scale carrying capacity, and the cable fissure self-test makes its unfinished tuning with stuff (no MakeThing error). |
| 2026-11-01 | Local build detects the installed game version: a plain build (Visual Studio or `dotnet build`) no longer assumes 1.6. It builds whichever version Steam has installed (Waylith.dll in the game folder means 1.7), saves that version's game DLLs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Rimshi\GameRefs\<version>, and also builds the other version from its saved copy, so switching to or from the 1.7 beta no longer breaks the build. build.ps1 uses the saved copies too; -p:RimWorldVersion and the CI build are unchanged. Same fix as Tithe PR #61. |
| 2026-11-01 | Skeletons are ancient: every newly generated skeleton is 120 to 180 years old biologically and 400 to 1200 years old chronologically. Skeletons never get the human old age conditions (frail, bad back, cataracts, dementia and the rest), whatever their age. Old Rim-Skeletons pawns converted by the mod setting are raised to at least 120 biologically and keep their chronological age. New self-tests check the ages and the missing old age conditions. |
| 2026-11-01 | Skeletons that lost their mechanical nature core (all of them in saves loaded after the machine organs update) get it back on load, with their machine organs. Skeleton organs read as machine parts again with Vanilla Races Expanded - Android loaded, a skeleton's human organs can never be harvested, and a regrown organ comes back as its machine part. On skeletons, Anesthetize is now "temporarily power down", which puts the unit in maintenance mode for hardware modifications; any anesthetic a skeleton would get is maintenance mode. |
| 2026-11-01 | The motor is now called the skeleton motor (item, bill and workbench text). Its def name is unchanged, so saves keep their motors. |
| 2026-11-01 | Storage: skeleton parts now have their own "skeleton parts" category inside prosthetics, and motors, robotic components and skeleton muscle have their own "skeleton components" category inside manufactured. Stockpiles that already allowed them keep allowing them. |
| 2026-10-31 | New building: the brass tuning fork, the first soft cycle facility for the skeleton repair bed. Built in the same room as a repair bed (up to two per bed), it burns one advanced component each time it joins a skeleton's soft cycle and makes that cycle 15% shorter. Costs 2 advanced components, 3 motors, 120 steel, 40 plasteel and 8 components, after the skeleton repair bed research. |
| 2026-10-31 | The brass tuning fork now stands upright on a stack of old stereo equipment, with gears threaded onto its tines, and draws power: 50 W idle and 1000 W while it rings for a soft cycle. Unpowered, it won't join a cycle. |
| 2026-10-31 | The brass tuning fork's stereo stack now has a tape deck at each end, joined by a band of audio tape, with the fork standing between them. |
| 2026-10-31 | Waking a skeleton early from a soft cycle in a repair bed, or losing the bed's power, no longer drops CPU integrity to 0% or overloads the CPU: it keeps what the cycle restored so far, but only a finished cycle has a finished cycle's effects. A soft cycle without a repair bed still resets to 0% if cut short. While a skeleton soft cycles, its other needs (maintenance, hygiene, recreation and the rest) no longer drop. The skeleton repair bed heals twice as fast. |
| 2026-10-31 | Skeleton remains are scrap, not meat. Butchery bills (butcher spot, butcher table, other mods) never take a skeleton corpse, and nothing eats one (pawns, animals, caravans); predators may still hunt skeletons but leave the body. New "scrap skeleton remains" bill at the crafting spot and machining table takes only skeleton corpses and yields 1-5 each of steel, plasteel and uranium and at most one component, with no meat, leather, blood or butchering mood. 5 new self-tests. |
| 2026-10-31 | Two new cores on every skeleton. Unshielded circuits: EMP now stuns skeletons the way it stuns mechanoids, and they briefly adapt after a stun like mechs do. Archaic speech: skeletons get worse trade prices (x0.5 price improvement), are weaker negotiators (x0.8) and convert others less well with Ideology (x0.8); the archaic persona's social impact penalty moved into it. Skeletons in existing saves get both cores when the save loads. |
| 2026-10-31 | A skeleton's genetics screen now calls its genes cores, subsets of the ancient AI core that runs it, and its xenogenes chips. The section headings, their tooltips, the View genes window and the character card tooltip all use the new words for skeletons only; everyone else's genetics screen is unchanged. Skeleton gene descriptions say "this core" too. |
| 2026-10-31 | Skeletons no longer have human organs. Their lungs, kidneys, stomach, liver, heart, eyes, ears and nose are now cooling fans, fluid reprocessors, a capacitor, an oil filter, a fluid pump, optical sensors, auditory arrays and an IR camera, and they're called that in the health tab, surgery menus, medical bills and the combat log. Each one can be made at the skeleton workbench for the same cost as a standard skeleton limb and installed with a repair kit once the old one is lost. Skeletons in an existing save get their machine organs when it loads. |
| 2026-10-31 | Wounded skeletons no longer show red flesh wounds. Injuries and lost parts on a skeleton are drawn with grey and black marks, the same kind mechanoids show; bionic eyes and other added-part overlays look as before. |
| 2026-10-31 | New building: the tempest reliquary (chemical spire), the second soft cycle facility for the skeleton repair bed. A 3x3 First Empire battery: fill it with 100 chemfuel, then keep it powered at 100 W for 36 hours straight, and it holds the charge forever. A skeleton finishing a soft cycle beside it takes the storm: immune to EMP stun until its next finished cycle, 10% faster until it uses a one-time electrostatic burst (radius 10, stuns and burns hostile mechanoids and skeletons, stronger with more total skill). One per repair bed. Costs 250 steel, 80 plasteel, 8 components and 2 advanced components, after the skeleton repair bed research. |
| 2026-10-31 | New building: the cable fissure, the third soft cycle facility for the skeleton repair bed. Before each use a researcher tunes it with a bill (4 components, 100 steel, 40 stone blocks, 250 research points of work); it draws 250 W until tuned and nothing after. A skeleton finishing a soft cycle beside a tuned fissure gets +20 carrying capacity until it uses a one-time heave: disarm one adjacent enemy and throw it carrying capacity / 10 cells, 2 to 5 damage on a different body part per cell, double for each cell a wall stops it short. One per repair bed. |
